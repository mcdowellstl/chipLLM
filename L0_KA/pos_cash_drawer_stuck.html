POS Cash Drawer Stuck Closed

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Insert the physical emergency release key into the small keyhole under the front or rear chassis of the drawer and rotate to pop open the drawer.
  2. Pull the cash drawer completely open and inspect the metal runners, tracks, and wheels for sticky buildup or lodged items.
  3. Trace the gray RJ12 drawer-kick cable connecting the drawer solenoid to the back of the POS receipt printer; verify it is fully clicked in.
  4. Test opening the drawer automatically by performing a standard cash transaction or a "No Sale" kick from the POS screen interface.

Verification Question: Does the cash drawer open automatically during a cash transaction?
