Reboot the Kiosk

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Locate the power button on the back panel of the kiosk cabinet.
  2. Hold for 10 seconds to force shutdown.
  3. Wait 30 seconds, then press once to restart.
  4. Wait for the welcome screen to appear (up to 3 minutes).

Verification Question: Is the kiosk showing the welcome screen?
