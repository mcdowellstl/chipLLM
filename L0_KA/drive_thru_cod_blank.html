Drive-Thru COD Outdoor Display Blank

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Verify that the outdoor lane COD circuit breakers inside the main power room panel are flipped to the ON position.
  2. Use the security key to open the outdoor COD panel enclosure at the back of the metal lane pedestal.
  3. Check that the internal digital media player box (e.g. Android or Linux media controller) has a solid green power LED.
  4. Cycle power to the media controller box by pulling its power adapter, waiting 15 seconds, and plugging it back in firmly.

Verification Question: Is the drive-thru outdoor COD screen now displaying the order menu?
