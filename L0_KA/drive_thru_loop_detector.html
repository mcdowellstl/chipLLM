Drive-Thru Loop Sensor / Detector Offline

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Locate the vehicle loop detector card rack chassis in the back office timer cabinet.
  2. Observe the loop status indicator; if blinking red or amber, it indicates a line fault or sensor loop open/short error.
  3. Press the physical tactile RESET button located on the front cover plate of the loop detector card.
  4. Conduct a lane check by driving a vehicle or rolling a metal cart directly over the loop sensor cuts in the lane pavement.

Verification Question: Does the loop detector amplifier board successfully light up when a vehicle passes?
