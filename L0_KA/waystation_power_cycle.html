Waystation Controller Unresponsive

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Locate the blue Waystation router controller module inside the main manager back office server rack cabinet.
  2. Observe the front status LEDs; confirm if the power light is steady blue, blinking, or completely unlit.
  3. If the device is frozen or displaying error lights, unplug the main black power adapter cable from the back of the Waystation unit.
  4. Wait 15 seconds, plug the power cable back in securely, and allow 5 minutes for the network routing tables to fully initialize.

Verification Question: Is the Waystation front panel showing a normal blue status indicator?
