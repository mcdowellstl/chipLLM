Kitchen Grill Printer Garbled Output

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Turn off the grill slip printer using the physical power toggle switch.
  2. Disconnect both the black power adapter cord and the interface cable (ethernet or serial) from the rear of the printer.
  3. Wait at least 30 seconds to allow the internal RAM microchip buffer inside the printer unit to completely discharge.
  4. Plug all connection cables back in, flip the power switch ON, and request a fresh grill ticket reprint from the KVS controller screen.

Verification Question: Did the printer output a clean, readable text slip?
