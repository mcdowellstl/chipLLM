Automated Beverage System Cup Jam

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Press the PAUSE button on the ABS machine main touch screen control panel to halt automated movements.
  2. Locate the specific cup dispenser turret chute showing a solid red light or a cup jam alert.
  3. Carefully pull out the nested cup or crumpled paper cup at the bottom opening of the dispenser throat to clear the blockage.
  4. Tap the RESUME button on the touch panel screen, then trigger a manual cup dispensing test to verify smooth operation.

Verification Question: Did the ABS dispense a single cup successfully without jam sensors flagging?
