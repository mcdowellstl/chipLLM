Internal Jam — Roller Check

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Power off the printer.
  2. Use a flashlight to inspect the paper path — look for small torn bits of paper.
  3. Use tweezers to remove any fragments found.
  4. Clean the paper sensor (small clear tab near the roller) with a dry cotton swab.
  5. Reload paper and power on. Try a test print.
