Drive-Thru Headset Pairing & Static Resolution

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Power off the static-filled headset and remove the slide-in rechargeable battery.
  2. Clean the metal copper contacts inside the battery cavity and on the battery itself with a clean swab to remove dirt or grease.
  3. Install a fresh, fully charged battery from the wall charging rack.
  4. Stand near the main drive-thru base station hub, press and hold the pairing button on the headset until you hear a voice confirm "paired".

Verification Question: Is the audio in the drive-thru headset clear without static?
