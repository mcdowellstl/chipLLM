Network / Wi-Fi Reset

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Locate the router or access point (usually mounted near the manager's office or above the POS area).
  2. Unplug the power cable from the router for 60 seconds.
  3. Plug it back in and wait 2 minutes for it to fully reconnect.
  4. Test connectivity on the POS terminal.

Verification Question: Is connectivity restored?
