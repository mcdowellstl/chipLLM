Smart Kitchen Controller Offline

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Locate the Smart Kitchen Controller (SKC) hub box which is typically mounted beneath the kitchen KVS monitor bracket assembly.
  2. Check the ethernet network link port on the SKC unit; verify if the amber and green link lights are blinking or completely unlit.
  3. Disconnect the primary ethernet line cable, blow out any grease or dust from the plug, and plug it back in firmly.
  4. Reboot the local controller by carefully pressing the small recessed red reset button on the housing with an open paperclip.

Verification Question: Does the KVS kitchen screen successfully connect to the master kitchen server?
