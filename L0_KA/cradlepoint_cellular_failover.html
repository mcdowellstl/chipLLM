Cradlepoint Cellular Signal Loss

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Locate the black Cradlepoint router unit, which is typically mounted high on the back office office wall near the ceiling.
  2. Verify the signal strength signal bar LEDs on the router front panel; check if they are lit green, flashing amber, or fully dark.
  3. Locate the multi-band cellular antennas on the back; un-thread them completely, blow away any dust, and screw them back on firmly.
  4. Perform a complete hard reboot of the Cradlepoint router by unplugging its DC power adapter connector brick, waiting 15 seconds, and reconnecting.

Verification Question: Are the Cradlepoint cellular signal strength LEDs lit green?
