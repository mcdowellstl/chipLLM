KVS Bump Bar Buttons Unresponsive

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Unplug the RJ45 ethernet-style or USB cable from the back connector of the KVS bump bar casing.
  2. Inspect the interface port and pins closely for grease buildup, kitchen moisture, or bent copper contacts.
  3. Clean the internal connector using a brief spray of electrical contact cleaner or compressed air.
  4. Securely reconnect the cable until a distinct locking click is heard, then reboot the associated kitchen display terminal.
